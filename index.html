About the model ⓘ

About this map

Map colours are modelled shortwave solar radiation, not measured street temperature. Weather comes from the hourly ERA5-Seamless grid; the model assumes 20% transmission through covered links and 100% outdoors. Covered is not zero exposure; areas outside the mapped network are not evaluated.

Each day 10,000 pedestrians depart between 09:00 and 19:00 — an uncalibrated high-demand scenario, not observed traffic. Pedestrians, demand and congestion are simulated. Both scenarios compare the same trips; routes are computed first and then replayed, and walking inside stations is excluded. Building footprints come from OSM; heights mix estimates and defaults, and buildings are not used to compute real shadows. The network and pedestrians are shown on an illustrative ground plane, not real elevation.

Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. Both views stay synchronised.
